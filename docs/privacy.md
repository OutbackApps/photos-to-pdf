# Photos to PDF privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

This policy covers **Photos to PDF**, an add-on for ServiceM8 developed by **Outback Apps** ("we", "us"). It explains what ServiceM8 account data the add-on uses, what else it collects, and how that data is handled. Photos to PDF is not made or supported by ServiceM8; ServiceM8's own [privacy policy](https://www.servicem8.com/privacy-policy) covers ServiceM8 itself.

## In short

Photos to PDF works within your ServiceM8 account. It reads a job's photos and details, makes the PDFs in your web browser or the ServiceM8 app, and saves them back to the job. **We don't keep copies of your photos, PDFs or job details, and we don't have a database of your data.**

## What ServiceM8 data it uses

When you connect Photos to PDF, you allow it to read jobs, read attachments and add attachments, in your ServiceM8 account. When you use it on a job, it uses:

- **The job's details** (such as its job number, PO number and address), to name the PDFs.
- **The job diary's photos**, to put in the PDFs.
- **Who you are in ServiceM8** (your staff and account identifiers), so the PDFs are added to the job under your name.
- **The PDF name setting** you choose in ServiceM8's add-on preferences.

It only uses this data to make your PDFs and save them to the job you're working on. It doesn't use it for anything else.

## Where the data goes

- **Your device:** the photos are loaded into the Photos to PDF window and made into PDFs there, in your browser or the ServiceM8 app.
- **ServiceM8:** the PDFs, and any photos you add from your device, are saved to the job in your ServiceM8 account. The add-on's code runs on ServiceM8's add-on hosting. If your device can't fetch a photo or save a PDF itself, it's passed through that hosting, within ServiceM8. Nothing is kept there.
- **Nobody else:** we don't sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers or analytics services.

## What else is collected

- **Settings on your device:** your choices in the window (Simple or Detailed, photos per PDF, Enlarge on hover) and a note of the largest PDF ServiceM8 accepted are remembered in your browser's local storage on that device. They contain no job or customer data, and stay on your device.
- **Logs:** ServiceM8's add-on hosting keeps short technical logs when Photos to PDF runs: your account, job and photo identifiers, the PDF name setting, sizes, and any errors. They don't contain photos, PDFs or customer details. We use them only to fix problems and support you, and ServiceM8 keeps them for a limited time.
- **Fonts:** the window loads its font (Open Sans) from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address as part of that request, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Photos to PDF uses no cookies, advertising or analytics.

## Keeping and deleting data

Because we don't store your photos, PDFs or job details, there's nothing of yours for us to delete when you stop using Photos to PDF. To stop it accessing your account, deactivate it in ServiceM8's Add-ons settings. The PDFs it saved stay in your job diaries until you delete them in ServiceM8. The settings on your device are cleared when you clear your browser's site data.

If you'd like us to delete anything relating to you, such as support emails, email us and we'll do so.

## Security

Photos to PDF uses ServiceM8's own sign-in (OAuth) and short-lived access tokens, and only the permissions it needs. It never asks for or sees your ServiceM8 password. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

## Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the privacy regulator where you live; in Australia, that's the [Office of the Australian Information Commissioner](https://www.oaic.gov.au/).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
