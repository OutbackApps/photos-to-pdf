# Add-on Store listing

The details to enter in the ServiceM8 developer account (Add-on Store Details) when submitting, written to ServiceM8's [Add-on Store Requirements](https://developer.servicem8.com/docs/addon-store-requirements). Not published on the help site.

## Name

Photos to PDF

## Short description

Turn a job's photos into PDFs saved to the job diary. Choose and order the photos, name the PDFs from the job's details, and save.

## Long description

Photos to PDF puts a job's photos into PDFs and saves them to the job diary, ready to attach to an email, a quote or a report.

Open a job and tap Photos to PDF: all the job diary's photos are shown, oldest first, already split into PDFs of up to 50 photos. Leave out the ones you don't need and drag the rest into order, then save. Each page is one photo, filling the page and the right way up, compressed a little so the PDFs stay a sensible size while keeping enough detail to zoom in on.

- Simple view: one list of photos to reorder, saved as PDFs of up to your chosen number of photos.
- Detailed view: choose exactly which photos go in each PDF.
- PDF names come from a template using the job's details, such as "Job #1042 Photos - PO 7781". Set your business's template in Settings → Preferences → Add-ons, or change it when saving. Each PDF can have its own name.
- Add photos from your device or camera as you go: they're added to the job diary too.
- Photos that are exact copies of another in the job are tagged as duplicates.
- PDFs are saved under the staff member's name. Large PDFs are split into parts automatically.

Works on the ServiceM8 online dashboard and in the ServiceM8 iPhone and iPad app.

$4.99 AUD a month, billed through ServiceM8.
